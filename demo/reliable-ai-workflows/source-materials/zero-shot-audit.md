# Zero-shot compilation audit

No private or supplied course content was used. The course was built from a learning goal and registered official sources.

## Decisions

- Keep the course vendor-neutral; use vendor documentation only for general patterns or current eval mechanics.
- Treat workflow/agent terminology as useful design distinctions rather than universal standards.
- Mark product, security, and risk-framework guidance time-sensitive and show learner-visible retrieval dates.
- Create the workflow canvas and teaching examples as `ai-synthesis` (`S05`), not as externally authoritative claims.
- Exclude production implementation and compliance claims from this short demo.

## Known limitations

- Two checkpoints cannot cover organization change, data governance, model selection, procurement, or production observability in depth.
- Current official guidance will need refresh as APIs, agent capabilities, and security taxonomies evolve.
- The course teaches design reasoning; it does not certify that a proposed workflow is safe or production-ready.
