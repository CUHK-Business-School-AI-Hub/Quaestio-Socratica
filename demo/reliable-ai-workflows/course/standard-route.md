# Standard route

Version 1.0 is the frozen baseline. Personal differences belong in `learner/personalized-route.md`.

## CP01 — From a good answer to a designed workflow

Estimated focused time: 100 minutes.

Nodes: `N01 → N02 → N03 → N04`.

The learner begins with a one-off AI task and discovers why prompt quality alone cannot guarantee repeatability. They define an observable outcome, decompose the work, and place gates and approvals.

Completion condition: the learner can produce a minimal workflow sketch and explain where probabilistic judgment ends and deterministic or human control begins.

Transition: CP02 asks what happens when this neat diagram encounters stale data, hostile inputs, tool permissions, and repeated use.

## CP02 — Reliability, safety, evaluation, and transfer

Estimated focused time: 100 minutes.

Nodes: `N05 → N06 → N07 → N08`.

The learner selects justified augmentations, threat-models the workflow, designs outcome-based eval cases, and completes a reliable workflow canvas for a real task.

Completion condition: the workflow has a clear outcome contract, bounded inputs, steps, gates, permissions, evidence, failure handling, test cases, and stopping conditions.

Course exit: ask about final output languages, then generate the personalized Cornell notes and offline mindmap.
